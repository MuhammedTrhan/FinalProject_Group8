The Cleaner (Neat Freak)
This guy pays way too much attention to his clothing and keeping everything spotless. He walks super slowly and systematically, just muttering to himself about dirt. The trick to getting something off his pockets is to purposely drop a dirty item to the ground. He hates messes so much that he literally freezes in place for 30 seconds just to clean it up. But whatever you do, do not hold a dirty item anywhere in his line of sight, or he triggers an immediate lockdown.
